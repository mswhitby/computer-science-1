# Computer Science I · Reusable Challenge Bank

This is a **separate track** from the sequential [Units](../units/) curriculum. Challenges are reusable questions, not lessons students must take in sequence.

- Each bank item has a stable folder named by its unique ID: `bank/<challenge-id>/`.
- `catalog.json` records **topic tags**, title, and a starting difficulty. A question can belong to multiple topics.
- `assignments/2026-10-26.json` defines this week's assigned questions, **difficulty and point values for this assignment**, without permanently tying the question itself to that difficulty.
- The original October 26 challenge repository is still the authoritative **submission and automated-grading destination**. Bank copies are classroom-display references.
- Skill guides are provided alongside three questions and can be expanded over time.

**[Open the classroom challenge website](https://www.mswhitby.com/computer-science-1/challenges/)** (hosted under the approved school domain).

For now, browser Run and Check are practice only. Click **Copy code**, then paste into the matching `solution.py` in your fork of [this week's challenge repository](https://github.com/mswhitby/python_problems_20261026) and open or update your pull request. That repository's grader and leaderboard remain in use.

## Future organization

Prefer topic tags and assignment manifests over physically moving questions between difficulty folders. That allows a problem to be "Hard" this month but "Easy" after students have practiced the underlying concepts, without breaking links or grading paths.
