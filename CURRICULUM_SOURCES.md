# Curriculum sources and reuse policy

The Computer Science I Python unit sequence uses the progression of the open textbook *How to Think Like a Computer Scientist: Learning with Python* by Jeffrey Elkner, Peter Wentworth, Allen B. Downey, and Chris Meyers, and the interactive ThinkCSPy edition by Runestone Interactive.

- [Original book (Open Book Project)](https://openbookproject.net/thinkcs/python/english3e/)
- [ThinkCSPy interactive edition](https://runestone.academy/ns/books/published/thinkcspy/index.html/)
- [Learn Python the Right Way](https://github.com/ritza-co/learn-python-the-right-way) — consulted as an example of learner-friendly organization, not copied from because third-party contributions need separate permission verification.

**Copyright practice:** Units 07–10 have new examples, original wording, and original exercises. Their subject sequence and Python facts are not copied textbook expression. Do not paste textbook prose, diagrams or modified examples into this repo without tracking the covered text's source, version, license, authors and any GFDL requirements. Any future genuine adaptation should carry its own complete licensing notices and required document sections.

**Curriculum integrity:** The Playground awards verified points against persisted course/activity IDs in graded submission records. Once an activity is published, do not rename/recycle its ID or silently change a graded contract. Supersede it with a new ID, and archive the old item without deleting its record, markdown or historical scoring metadata.
